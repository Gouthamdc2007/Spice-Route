Our Mission

Rooted in Tradition, Delivered Hot to You

Spice Route was founded in 2008 in the historic lanes of Old Delhi with a mission to bring royal Indian dining directly to people's homes. We believe that delivery food should never compromise on taste, authenticity, or presentation.

Our master chefs prepare every order fresh upon receipt, using time-tested brass and clay handi cooking methods, pure ghee, and whole spices ground daily in-house.

Every dish is packed in eco-friendly, heat-retaining containers and delivered across Delhi NCR by our dedicated delivery fleet.
